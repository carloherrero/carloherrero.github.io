CPA Intake Assistant

Document intake for small CPA firms, by Carlo Herrero

CPA Intake Assistant helps tax and accounting firms collect client documents by email. It reads a dedicated intake mailbox, identifies which tax document each attachment is (for example, a W-2 or a 1099), matches it to the right client, and shows staff a checklist of what has arrived and what is still missing.

The current version is a demonstration. It connects only to a demo mailbox and uses test data.
